- Incubated and launched three platform products covering the shared service pillars of third-party data ingestion, data attribute governance and fast-track deployment of the Viva suite.
- **[Microsoft 365 People Skills](/work/people-skills/).** Started as a skunkworks hack with three engineers, came out of the Viva stable with an M365-scale intent, and shipped as the skills layer of Microsoft 365 Copilot in 2025. It grew into the People & Work Intelligence charter I lead today.
- **Feature Access Management.** There were feature-flag services and identity-bound access control, but nothing in between: a high-throughput, inline policy evaluator. We needed it for Viva and built it for all of Microsoft 365. Viva, Copilot Analytics, Viva Insights and many privacy-sensitive Copilot features use it today.
- **Employee Self-Service Agent.** Incubated in my group, which built the v0 shown in preview at Ignite 2024, before it moved to a dedicated team.
