Most AI training asks people to stop working so they can learn how AI could help them work. Then they go back to their desk and do things the old way. Learning Agent flips that. It is a Copilot agent that helps people build AI skills while they work, inside the task in front of them.

My AI Upskilling group built it. It went GA in June 2026, and a new wave of features shipped at Ignite 2026.

## What We Believed

- Learning belongs in the flow of work, not in a separate destination.
- It needs an agentic, assistive front door.
- It has to be personal, grounded in each person's role and AI fluency.
- AI upskilling is what keeps the AI transformation flywheel spinning.

## How It Works

It starts from what we already understand about the person: their role, their skills from [People Skills](/work/people-skills/), the tasks they do and their context in Work IQ. From there it offers AI tips, task-based learning, prompt coaching on scenarios from their own work, role play, skill assessments and learning plans.

Content comes from AI Skills Navigator, Copilot Academy and the organization's own learning catalog. Admins can add their AI usage policy to onboarding, assign training and track completion.

## Where My Two Groups Meet

Learning Agent is where People & Work Intelligence and AI Upskilling come together. The understanding of people and work we build for Copilot is what lets the agent meet someone in the task they are already doing. Without it, we would be sending them to a course catalog again.
