I lead two groups. **PWI: People & Work Intelligence** is one of the producers of intelligence that goes into Work IQ, Copilot, Copilot memory and Copilot and FinOps analytics. **AI Upskilling** builds [Learning Agent](/work/learning-agent/) and runs Viva Learning.

What PWI powers, as a customer would notice it:

- **Expertise matching.** When Copilot works out the best person to ask about a project, topic or system, our understanding of people and their work is at the core of that match.
- **People cards.** Hover over anyone's people card in a Microsoft 365 app, and the skill tags you see come from [People Skills](/work/people-skills/).
- **Skills landscape.** Leaders can see their organization's skills profile and where to invest in upskilling.
- **Task and workflow intelligence.** Copilot can answer questions like "what is the most common type of work my team does".
- **What people use AI for.** We produce the categorization and the taxonomy behind analytics on the kinds of tasks people bring to AI, for a company or an org.
- **AI value and consumption.** Task and task-completion intelligence, mapped to token cost, drives the usage page in the Copilot app and the org-level FinOps analytics in [Agent 365 Business Analytics](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/ai-finops-blog/introducing-microsoft-agent-365-business-analytics-turn-ai-investment-into-busin/4574149): AI measured by the work it gets done, not the tokens it burns.
