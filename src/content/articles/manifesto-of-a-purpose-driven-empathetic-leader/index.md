Purpose-driven, empathetic leadership is an infinite game and a constant endeavour requiring deliberate and thoughtful efforts. Leaders should be visionaries yet open to changes as they uncover new data and perspectives. Based on the following principles, I am committed to pushing myself to be a better leader every day and calibrating myself on the level of service I provide (to both the people in my span of care and myself).

![Photo by Duane Storey on Unsplash](./images/image-01.jpeg "Photo by Duane Storey on Unsplash")

## To Serve Selflessly

> My moral compass will always point to what is in the best interest of people in my span of care. I would never make any decision with either the thought of what is in it for me or the fear of how I might be judged for this decision. I believe that leadership is a position of service and that service mindset is my source of engagement/job satisfaction.

## Pursuit of Purpose

> As humans, our work-related purpose and vision are interlinked to our aspirations and state of mind. I will strive to nurture the aspirations by matching goals to your strengths so that it catalyzes your connection to the product’s purpose. I am grounded in the thought that I can’t expect you to commit to the vision by default, and it’s my responsibility to build a working environment that allows you to embrace the product’s vision.

## Inclusive and Empathetic to Human Variances

> I will commit to building a team culture that allows you to bring your whole self to work and will always personalize communication and expectation per individual. I am affective to the diversity and uniqueness in us. I believe that thought diversity is what makes us stronger as a group.

## Model Self Compassion

> I will be kind to myself and practice personal sustainability to set an example for everyone in my span of care. I will always prioritize your personal sustainability and continued engagement in the long-term over any short-term gain, irrespective of the pressure that deliverables may carry.

## Provide Psychological Safety and Empowerment

> I want each team member to feel empowered to make decisions and own outcomes without any fear of failure. This is easier said than done. The only way to achieve this is to provide psychological safety to every team member through inclusive communication, blame-free risk-taking, and holding a high bar for empathetic communication standards. As your leader, I will trust by default, strive to earn your trust every day and offer help whenever you need it.

## The buck stops here

> It’s my responsibility to create visibility to all the remarkable things you do for the customers. When things don’t go as planned, and in case of failures — I will take ownership of the outcome without deflecting any blame or searching for a place to hide.

## Embrace entropy & prevent chaos

> Entropy and prioritization changes are constant in a business. I will strive to build the team’s resilience to dealing with entropy while safeguarding them from misaligned entropy that leads to chaos.

## Make myself redundant

> My primary job is to create and grow more leaders to the point that I make myself or my role redundant. I will commit to doing this with open vulnerability as I believe this will empower my team to grow without restriction and never feel that there is a hierarchy to the span of responsibility in decision-making.
